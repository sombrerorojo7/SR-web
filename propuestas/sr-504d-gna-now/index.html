Executive Summary

Overview

Greater National Advocates (GNA Now) has operated the nation's leading directory of Independent Patient Advocates since 2018 — connecting patients and families with professionals who work solely for them. The mission is compelling, the patient testimonials are powerful, and the need is real and growing.

The opportunity: GNA Now's digital presence is not yet converting that trust and mission into consistent visibility, audience engagement, or guidance requests. This report documents specific findings from an initial review of GNA Now's social media channels and website, and presents two concrete proposals to address that gap.

💡 Largest Untapped Opportunity: Local SEO at Scale

GNA Now has advocates in every U.S. state but no state- or city-specific landing pages. Searches like "patient advocate Chicago" or "independent patient advocate Texas" represent high-intent queries with relatively limited competition. Properly structured landing pages per state — with schema markup and local keyword integration — could significantly increase organic visibility over 6–12 months.
